# Mobile app error codes

The Acme Outfitters mobile app shows short error codes. Error E-1042 means the payment was declined by the bank; ask the customer to try another card. Error E-2210 means the session has expired and the customer must sign in again. Error E-3307 means the item is out of stock at the selected store; suggest a nearby store or home delivery. Any other error should be reported to the support desk with a screenshot.
