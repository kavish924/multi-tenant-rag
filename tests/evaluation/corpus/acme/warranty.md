# Warranty

Tents and backpacks carry a two-year warranty against manufacturing defects such as broken zippers, failed seams and cracked buckles. To file a claim, complete the warranty form on the website with your order number and photos of the defect. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, UV fading, or damage from misuse. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at our choice, and replacement items ship free of charge.
