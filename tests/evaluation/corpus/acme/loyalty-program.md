# TrailPoints loyalty program

Members of TrailPoints earn one point for every dollar spent in stores and online. Every 500 points convert into a 10 dollar reward certificate. Points expire after 18 months without any purchase on the account. Members who earn 2,000 points in a calendar year reach Gold tier, which includes free express shipping on every order and early access to seasonal sales. Joining TrailPoints is free and only needs an email address.
