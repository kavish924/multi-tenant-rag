# Gear rentals

The Denver flagship store rents camping and paddling gear. Two-person tents rent for 25 dollars per day and kayaks rent for 45 dollars per day, including a paddle and life jacket. Every rental requires a refundable deposit of 200 dollars, held on a credit card. Gear returned after closing time is charged one extra rental day. Rentals must be reserved at least 48 hours in advance through the website.
