# Shipping

Standard shipping takes three to five business days and is free on orders over 50 dollars. Express shipping arrives the next business day and costs 14.99 dollars. Tents and other oversized items cannot be shipped to PO boxes. Acme Outfitters does not offer international shipping at this time. Orders placed before 2 pm Mountain Time leave the warehouse the same day. Every shipment includes a tracking number sent by email.
