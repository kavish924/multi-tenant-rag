# Refund policy

Acme Outfitters accepts returns of unopened items within 30 days of delivery. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within five business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. Opened items can be exchanged for store credit at the store manager's discretion. A receipt or the online order number is required for every return. Staff record approved returns under the internal reason code RFD-30. Items bought on clearance are final sale and cannot be returned. Shipping charges from the original order are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged.
