# Incident severity levels

SEV1 is a customer-facing outage or data loss: the on-call engineer must be paged within five minutes, and the public status page is updated every 30 minutes until resolution. SEV2 is a major degradation with a workaround, updated every two hours. SEV3 is a minor issue handled during business hours. Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident gets a written postmortem within five business days.
